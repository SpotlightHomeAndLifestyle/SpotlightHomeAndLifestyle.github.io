A Private Primary Suite for a Growing Family

The goal of this project was to create more space, privacy and functionality for a growing family while allowing them to remain in the home and location they loved. With all of the existing bedrooms grouped together, only one bathroom and a need for dedicated office space, the family wanted an addition that would make everyday routines easier while giving the homeowners a private retreat of their own. The solution was a new second-floor primary suite, complete with a spacious bedroom, walk-in closet and ensuite featuring a walk-in shower and additional bathroom storage. Designed with comfort, privacy and quality in mind, the addition gave the family the extra space they needed without sacrificing the home and community they already loved.
